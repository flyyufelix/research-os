Your first working system

From a rough question to a research compass

You do not need a polished proposal. Start with what you know, what you suspect, and what you still need to learn.

01

Add your starting material

Write rough ideas in staging-material/ideas.md. Papers and personal notes are optional.

02

Open the project in an agent

Use ChatGPT Work, Tencent WorkBuddy, QwenWork, Doubao Work, or another agent you prefer with the project folder.

03

Build the manifesto

The included workflow maps your material and drafts manifesto.md for your review.

04

Scout the literature

Search for work that supports, fills, or challenges the gaps in your current research direction.
