# Study-session journal

## Dense reading

When I read a difficult journal article, familiar songs with lyrics make it easier to begin but I often reread sentences. Silence feels less pleasant at first, yet I usually finish the argument faster.

## Repetitive work

Instrumental music seems useful when I am formatting references or organizing files. The work feels less tedious, and I do not notice the same competition for words.

## What I want to test

I should not rely on these impressions as evidence. A useful study would compare the same students across silence, instrumental music, and lyrical music while measuring:

- comprehension or recall;
- time on task;
- willingness to continue;
- perceived concentration;
- familiarity with and preference for the music.

My current hunch is that music may improve the experience of studying without improving learning. That distinction matters more to me than finding one winning playlist.
