# Literature Scout seen-item ledger

This ledger prevents repeated recommendations and records human decisions. Preserve existing rows. Use stable identifiers whenever possible.

| Identifier | First reported | Last seen | Status | Title | Report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Valid statuses are `reported`, `indexed`, `watch`, and `dismissed`. Literature Scout creates only `reported`; the update workflow records the other transitions after an explicit action or successful ingestion.
